Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Baams Away handles information when you use the game on iOS, Android, or another supported platform.

Effective July 14, 2026

Baams Away is developed and operated by Bearded Eye (“Baams Away,” “we,” “our,” or “us”). We respect your privacy and aim to collect only the information reasonably necessary to operate, support, secure, and improve the game.

Information we may collect

Depending on the features available in the version you use, we may collect:

  • Gameplay progress, achievements, settings, and preferences.
  • Basic device and app information, such as device type, operating system, language, region, and app version.
  • Crash reports, error logs, and performance diagnostics made available through Apple, Google, or the operating system.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting us for support.
  • Account or profile information only if account-based features are introduced and you choose to use them.
Baams Away does not currently use Firebase or a third-party advertising network.

How we use information

We may use information to:

  • Operate and maintain the game.
  • Save or restore gameplay progress where supported.
  • Diagnose crashes, bugs, and performance issues.
  • Improve gameplay, stability, accessibility, and compatibility.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Protect the game and players from misuse, fraud, or security threats.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

Platform services

Baams Away may interact with services provided by Apple or Google, such as the App Store, Google Play, Game Center, platform-based cloud storage, purchases, achievements, or other operating-system features. Those companies may process information under their own privacy policies and account settings.

Purchases

If Baams Away offers in-app purchases, payments are processed by Apple, Google, or the applicable platform provider. We do not receive or store your full payment-card information.

Advertising and sale of personal information

Baams Away does not currently use third-party advertising networks. We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Sharing of information

We may share limited information only when reasonably necessary:

  • With platform providers or service providers that help us operate or support the game.
  • To comply with law, legal process, or valid government requests.
  • To protect the rights, safety, and security of our players, our services, or the public.
  • As part of a business transfer, such as a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law.

Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including operating the game, resolving support issues, maintaining security, and complying with legal obligations.

Children’s privacy

Baams Away is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate authorization, contact us and we will review and delete it where required.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of personal information associated with you. You may also have the right to object to or restrict certain processing, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To make a request, visit our Privacy Choices page or email zach@beardedeye.com.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

International users

Baams Away may be available in multiple countries. Information may be processed in the United States or other locations where our platform providers or service providers operate, subject to applicable law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game, our practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
