Advertising choices

Iguaninja and Pals may show ads through third-party advertising networks. You can limit or opt out of personalized advertising by using your device's built-in controls:

  • iOS: Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking to control app tracking permissions, or turn off Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising → Personalized Ads.
  • Android: Go to Settings → Google → Ads to opt out of personalized ads or reset your advertising ID.

Where required by law, we also honor browser- or device-level opt-out preference signals, such as Global Privacy Control (GPC), and we present a consent option before using non-essential advertising or analytics technologies for players in regions such as the EEA, UK, and Switzerland.

Available requests

Depending on your location and the information associated with your use of Iguaninja and Pals, you may request that we:

  • Confirm whether we hold personal information associated with you.
  • Provide access to or a copy of that information.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete personal information, subject to applicable exceptions.
  • Explain how information is used or shared, including with advertising partners.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for advertising purposes.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing where applicable.

How to submit a request

Email zach@beardedeye.com with the subject line Iguaninja and Pals Privacy Request.

Please include only the information needed to locate and verify your request, such as:

  • Your player name or account identifier, if one exists.
  • The email address associated with your account, if applicable.
  • The platform you use, such as iOS or Android.
  • A clear description of the request.
Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Verification and response

We may ask for reasonable information to verify your identity and protect player data. We will respond within the time required by applicable law. Some requests may be limited where retention is required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or other lawful purposes.

Platform controls

Some privacy settings are controlled directly through Apple, Google, your device settings, Game Center, Google Play Games, or the applicable app marketplace. You may need to use those platform controls to manage platform-level data, permissions, or ad tracking.

Questions
